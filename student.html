WEEK 1 WRITING

Start with strokes, then write 三 十 八 九

First learn the direction of five basic strokes. Then see how those strokes come together in four simple number characters. Choose Watch first, then Try It.

Remember: write from top to bottom and left to right. When a horizontal and vertical stroke cross, the horizontal stroke usually comes first.
